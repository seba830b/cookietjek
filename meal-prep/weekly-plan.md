# Weekly plan

One prep session, about **60 min**. Covers **10 meals** (5 days, lunch + dinner) across 4 flavor styles: Mexican, Italian, Indian, Korean.

Prep on Sunday. Mon-Wed boxes go in the fridge, Thu-Fri boxes go in the freezer so nothing sits in the fridge longer than 3 days.

## The week

| Day | Lunch | Dinner |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Chicken burrito bowl (Mexican) | Pasta al forno (cheesy bolognese bake) (Italian) |
| Tue | Butter chicken (Indian) | Taco loaded fries (Mexican) |
| Wed | Korean sticky chicken bowl (Korean) | Chicken burrito bowl (Mexican) |
| Thu | Pasta al forno (cheesy bolognese bake) (Italian) ❄ | Butter chicken (Indian) ❄ |
| Fri | Korean sticky chicken bowl (Korean) ❄ | Cheesy beef quesadillas (Mexican) ❄ |

❄ = frozen on prep day.

- Wed evening: move the Thu boxes from freezer to fridge.
- Thu evening: move the Fri boxes from freezer to fridge.

### When you eat

- **Chicken burrito bowl:** Microwave 2½ min, then salsa + crème fraîche on top.
- **Pasta al forno (cheesy bolognese bake):** Microwave 2½ min, then airfryer 200°C 4-5 min for golden cheese.
- **Butter chicken:** Microwave 3-3½ min, stir halfway.
- **Taco loaded fries:** Wedges 200°C 15 min, top with hot mince + cheese, 2 min more.
- **Korean sticky chicken bowl:** Microwave 3 min. Spring onion on top if you bought it.
- **Cheesy beef quesadillas:** 2 tortillas, filling + cheese, airfryer 200°C 6-7 min, flip at 4.

## Prep day timeline

Four tracks run at the same time: the **POT** (rice, then pasta), the **PAN** (mince), the **AIRFRYER** (chicken) and **YOU** (cutting, mixing, boxing).
The combo can't microwave and airfry at once, so prep day uses no microwave at all.

| Time | Track | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| -1 day | YOU | Move the 1 kg frozen chicken to the fridge to thaw. Wash 10 boxes, at least 2 of them oven-safe glass for the pasta bake. |
| 0:00 | POT | Rinse 450 g rice, drain. Add 700 ml water + pinch of salt. Lid on, high heat. |
| 0:02 | YOU | Cut the chicken into 3 cm bites with scissors. Toss in a big bowl with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp paprika and 2 tbsp cornstarch. |
| 0:05 | POT | Rice boiling? Lowest heat, lid on, timer 12 min. |
| 0:08 | AIRFRYER | Chicken batch 1 (half), single layer. 200°C, 13 min. Shake at 7 min. |
| 0:09 | PAN | Medium-high, splash of oil. 500 g mince + 150 g frozen onion + 1 tsp salt. Break up, brown 8 min. |
| 0:17 | POT | Rice off the heat. Lid stays on for 10 min. |
| 0:17 | PAN | Scoop half the mince into a bowl (taco half). Pour 1 jar pasta sauce into the pan, simmer 5 min. That is your bolognese. |
| 0:21 | AIRFRYER | Batch 1 out, check no pink inside. Batch 2 in, 200°C, 13 min, shake at 7. |
| 0:22 | PAN | Bolognese into a bowl. Taco half back in the pan + 1 bag taco mix + 1 can drained kidney beans + 100 ml water. Bubble 4 min, then into a box. Pan is done. |
| 0:27 | POT | Spread the rice on a big plate to cool. Quick rinse, fill the pot with water + salt, lid on, high heat. |
| 0:28 | YOU | Korean sauce in a small bowl: 2 tbsp gochujang + 1 tbsp soy + 1 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp water. Label your boxes with tape: day + meal. |
| 0:33 | POT | 200 g penne in. Cook 1 min less than the pack says. |
| 0:34 | AIRFRYER | Batch 2 out. Airfryer is done. |
| 0:35 | YOU | Split the chicken in 3. Third 1: toss with ½ bag taco mix + 2 tbsp water. Third 2: toss with the Korean sauce. Third 3: into a bowl with 1 jar butter chicken sauce. |
| 0:42 | POT | Drain the pasta, back into the pot, stir in the bolognese. Into the 2 glass boxes, 100 g cheese on top. |
| 0:45 | YOU | Assemble the rice boxes (about 150 g rice each). Burrito x2: rice + taco chicken + ½ can corn + 60 g cheese. Butter chicken x2 and Korean x2: rice + chicken + 300 g frozen broccoli split over the 4 boxes. |
| 0:52 | YOU | Taco mince into 2 boxes: Tue fries, Fri quesadillas. Lids on loosely while it all steams off. |
| 0:55 | YOU | Fridge: Mon, Tue, Wed boxes. Freezer: Thu + Fri boxes. Everything cold within 1 hour of cooking. |
| 0:58 | YOU | Wash the pot, pan and bowls. Done. Go lie down. |

Cooking more than one person's worth? Every extra 500 g chicken is one more airfryer batch, about +13 min.
