# Lazy Prep

Low-effort comfort food meal prep. Airfryer/microwave combo, 1 pan, 1 pot, no oven.

- `index.html` - open on your phone. Plan, recipes and shopping list in one page, checkboxes saved on the device, portion slider.
- `weekly-plan.md` - 60 min prep, 10 meals, 4 flavor styles, step-by-step timeline.
- `recipes.md` - 5 base components + 10 dishes.
- `shopping-list.md` - indkøbsliste på dansk med priser (Netto, Rema 1000, Lidl).
- `starter-list.md` - alt til alle 10 opskrifter, holdbarhed og hvor i Svendborg.
