# Recipes

Lazy comfort food. Every recipe makes **2 portions** (lunch + dinner, or two days) and takes **10 min hands-on or less**.
Everything is built from 5 base components, so one prep session covers a bunch of dishes.

Gear: microwave/airfryer combo, 1 pan, 1 pot. No oven. Microwave times are for 800 W.

## Base components

### Base A: Crispy airfryer chicken

Makes 6 portions.

- 1 kg chicken breast, thawed (frozen bag is cheapest)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tbsp cornstarch (majsstivelse)

1. Cut into 3 cm bites. Use kitchen scissors straight over the bowl, no cutting board needed.
2. Toss everything in a big bowl until coated.
3. Airfryer 200°C, 12-14 min per batch (about 500 g), single layer. Shake at 7 min.
4. Done when the biggest piece has no pink inside (75°C if you own a thermometer).

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 3 months. Freeze flat in a bag so the bites don't clump.

> Cornstarch is the crispy trick. Don't skip it.

### Base B: Seasoned mince, 2 ways

Makes about 500 g taco mince + 650 g bolognese.

- 500 g beef mince 8-12%
- 150 g frozen chopped onion
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 jar pasta sauce (about 500 g)
- 1 bag taco spice mix
- 1 can kidney beans, drained
- 100 ml water

1. Pan, medium-high, splash of oil. Mince + onion + salt. Break it up with the spatula and brown for 8 min.
2. Scoop half into a bowl. That is your taco half.
3. Pasta sauce into the pan with the other half, simmer 5 min. That is bolognese. Tip it into a box.
4. Taco half back in the pan with taco mix, beans and water. Bubble 3-4 min until thick.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 3 months

> Cheeseburger version (recipe 10): skip both sauces and stir in 1 tbsp ketchup + 1 tsp paprika instead.

### Base C: Big pot of rice

Makes 6 portions.

- 450 g jasmine rice
- 700 ml water
- ½ tsp salt

1. Rinse the rice once in the pot, drain.
2. Add water + salt, lid on, high heat until it boils.
3. Lowest heat, 12 min. Don't lift the lid.
4. Off the heat, lid on, 10 min more.
5. Spread it on a big plate to cool fast. Fridge within 1 hour.

- **Fridge:** 3 days. Rice is the one to be strict about, cool it fast.
- **Freezer:** 2 months

> Shortcut: microwave rice pouches, 2 min at 800 W. Pricier, zero work.

### Base D: Pasta

Makes 2 portions.

- 200 g penne
- 1 tsp salt

1. Big pot of salted water, boil.
2. Cook 1 min LESS than the pack says. It softens more when you reheat it.
3. Drain and mix with sauce straight away so it doesn't glue together.

- **Fridge:** 3-4 days (with sauce)
- **Freezer:** 3 months (with sauce)

### Base E: Frozen potato wedges

Makes 2 portions.

- 500 g frozen potato wedges (kartoffelbåde)

1. Never prep these. Cook straight from frozen when you eat, it is zero effort anyway.
2. Airfryer 200°C, 15-18 min, shake at 8 min.

- **Fridge:** Don't. Always cook fresh.
- **Freezer:** That's where they live.

### 1. Chicken burrito bowl

**Mexican** · Bases A + C · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- ½ bag taco spice mix
- 2 tbsp water
- 300 g cooked rice (Base C)
- ½ can sweetcorn, drained
- 60 g grated cheese
- 4 tbsp salsa
- 2 tbsp crème fraîche

1. Toss the warm chicken with taco mix and water. It turns into a sticky coating.
2. Box it: rice, chicken, corn, cheese on top.
3. Salsa and crème fraîche stay in their jars until you eat.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 2 months, without salsa and crème fraîche
- **Reheat:** Microwave 800 W, lid loose, 2-2½ min. Then cold salsa + crème fraîche on top. Hot and cold together is the whole point.

### 2. Cheesy beef quesadillas

**Mexican** · Bases B · 6 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 4 large tortillas
- 500 g taco mince (Base B)
- 100 g grated cheese
- salsa + crème fraîche to dip

1. Warm the filling 1 min in the microwave so the cheese melts properly.
2. Filling + cheese on one half of each tortilla, fold.
3. Airfryer 200°C, 6-7 min, flip at 4 min. If they flap open, weigh them down with the rack.
4. Cut into triangles and dip.

- **Fridge:** Filling 3 days. Make the quesadillas fresh, it takes 7 min.
- **Freezer:** Filling 3 months. Or freeze them folded but uncooked, then airfry from frozen at 190°C for 10-12 min, flip halfway.
- **Reheat:** Never microwave a quesadilla, it goes floppy. Leftovers: airfryer 180°C, 3-4 min.

### 3. Taco loaded fries

**Mexican** · Bases B + E · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 500 g frozen potato wedges (Base E)
- 500 g taco mince (Base B)
- 80 g grated cheese
- 4 tbsp salsa
- 2 tbsp crème fraîche
- pickled jalapeños (optional)

1. Microwave the taco mince 2 min first, keep it covered.
2. Wedges in the airfryer, 200°C, 15 min, shake at 8.
3. Pile mince + cheese on the wedges (move to an airfryer-safe dish if your basket is a grill), 200°C, 2-3 min until melty.
4. Salsa, crème fraîche, jalapeños on top.

- **Fridge:** Mince 3 days. Fries are always cooked fresh.
- **Freezer:** Mince 3 months.
- **Reheat:** Not a reheat dish. Fresh fries are what keeps it crispy, and they are zero effort.

### 4. Butter chicken

**Indian** · Bases A + C · 3 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- 1 jar butter chicken sauce (350-400 g)
- 300 g cooked rice (Base C)
- 150 g frozen broccoli or peas
- frozen naan from Lidl (optional)

1. Chicken in the box, pour the sauce over, stir.
2. Rice on the other side. Frozen broccoli goes straight in, it cooks when you reheat.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 3 months. One of the best freezers on this list.
- **Reheat:** Microwave 800 W, lid loose, 3-3½ min, stir halfway. Dry rice? Splash 1 tbsp water on it first. Naan: airfryer 180°C, 2-3 min.

### 5. Korean sticky chicken bowl

**Korean** · Bases A + C · 4 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp water
- 300 g cooked rice (Base C)
- 150 g frozen broccoli
- spring onion + sesame seeds (optional)

1. Stir gochujang, soy, honey and water in a bowl. No gochujang? Use 2 tbsp sriracha + an extra ½ tbsp honey.
2. Toss in the chicken.
3. Box: rice, sticky chicken, frozen broccoli. Spring onion + sesame go on when you eat.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 2 months
- **Reheat:** Lazy: microwave 3 min, lid loose. Crispy: keep the sauce in a small cup, airfry the plain chicken 180°C 4 min, then toss.

> Korean beef variant: same sauce on plain browned mince.

### 6. Pasta al forno (cheesy bolognese bake)

**Italian** · Bases B + D · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 200 g penne, cooked (Base D)
- 650 g bolognese (Base B)
- 100 g grated cheese

1. Mix the drained pasta with the hot bolognese.
2. Split into boxes that can go in the airfryer (oven-safe glass). Cheese on top.

- **Fridge:** 3-4 days
- **Freezer:** 3 months. Thaw in the fridge overnight.
- **Reheat:** Microwave 800 W, lid on, 2½ min to heat through. Lid off, then airfryer 200°C, 4-5 min until the cheese bubbles and browns. That's the "baked" part, no oven needed.

### 7. Creamy garlic parmesan chicken pasta

**Italian** · Bases A + D · 6 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 200 g penne, cooked (Base D)
- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- 150 g cream cheese (flødeost naturel)
- 150 ml milk
- 40 g grated parmesan (Grana Padano)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 100 g frozen chopped spinach
- black pepper

1. Big microwave bowl: spinach, 2 min.
2. Add cream cheese, milk, garlic and parmesan. 1½ min, stir until smooth.
3. Toss in the hot pasta and chicken. Box it.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 1 month. The sauce may split a bit, stir in milk and it comes back.
- **Reheat:** Add 2 tbsp milk first, cream sauces thicken in the fridge. Microwave 2 min, stir, 30 s more.

### 8. Sweet & sour chicken

**Asian takeaway** · Bases A + C · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- 1 jar sweet & sour sauce (about 400 g)
- 250 g frozen wok vegetables
- 300 g cooked rice (Base C)

1. Microwave the wok veg in a covered bowl, 4 min. Pour off the water.
2. Mix veg + sauce + chicken.
3. Box it with rice.

- **Fridge:** 3 days
- **Freezer:** 2 months
- **Reheat:** Microwave 3 min, stir halfway. Takeaway-level crispy: store the chicken dry, airfry 180°C 4 min, toss in the hot sauce.

### 9. Buffalo chicken loaded fries

**American diner** · Bases A + E · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 500 g frozen potato wedges (Base E)
- 230 g crispy chicken (Base A)
- 4 tbsp hot sauce (Frank's style or sriracha)
- 1 tbsp butter
- 60 g grated cheddar
- 3 tbsp garlic dressing or ranch

1. Microwave butter + hot sauce 30 s, stir. That is buffalo sauce.
2. Wedges in the airfryer, 200°C, 15 min, shake at 8. Chicken on top for the last 4 min.
3. Toss the chicken in the sauce, back on the fries, cheese over, 1 more min.
4. Dressing on top.

- **Fridge:** Chicken 3 days. Fries always fresh.
- **Freezer:** Chicken 3 months.
- **Reheat:** Nothing to reheat. Everything is cooked fresh in about 20 min, 5 of them hands-on.

### 10. Cheeseburger fries bowl

**American diner** · Bases B + E · 5 min hands-on · 2 portions

- 500 g frozen potato wedges (Base E)
- 250 g cheeseburger mince (Base B, see note)
- 60 g grated cheddar
- 3 tbsp burger dressing
- 6 pickle slices, chopped

1. Microwave the mince 1½ min, keep it covered.
2. Wedges in the airfryer, 200°C, 15 min, shake at 8.
3. Mince + cheese over the wedges, 200°C, 2 min until melted.
4. Dressing + pickles on top. Basically a Big Mac with fries, in a bowl.

- **Fridge:** Mince 3 days. Fries always fresh.
- **Freezer:** Mince 3 months.
- **Reheat:** Cook the fries fresh. Only the mince gets reheated.

## Reheat rules

- Microwave times are for 800 W. At 1000 W take off about 20%.
- Lid on loosely in the microwave. Steam keeps sauces saucy and rice soft.
- Anything that should be crispy finishes in the airfryer, 180-200°C for 3-5 min. Never just microwave it.
- Combo oven: no foil, no metal trays in microwave mode. Glass and ceramic are fine in both modes.
- Thaw frozen boxes in the fridge overnight. Forgot? Microwave on defrost 4-5 min, then reheat as normal.
- Stir halfway. Microwaves heat edges first, stirring kills the cold middle.
